# Work From Home Equipment Policy

**Policy version:** 1.0
**Effective date:** 1 October 2026
**Authority:** WorkRight Labs Sdn. Bhd.

> Fictional company policy for the WorkRight learning project.

# 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out the equipment and allowances WorkRight Labs provides to
employees who work from home under an approved flexible working arrangement
(FWA) that changes their place of work.

It applies to active full-time employees in Peninsular Malaysia and the
Federal Territory of Labuan whose approved FWA includes at least two
work-from-home days per week. Employees on probation are not eligible until
their probation is confirmed.

# 2. Equipment entitlement

Eligible employees may claim the following:

- One company laptop (standard issue; replaced every 4 years)
- One external monitor (up to RM 800, replaced every 5 years)
- One ergonomic chair (one-time stipend of RM 500)
- Monthly internet allowance of RM 100, paid with salary

The internet allowance continues while the approved FWA remains active and
stops in the month after the arrangement ends.

# 3. Claiming and reimbursement

Claims are submitted through the employee's manager with receipts attached.
Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of purchase. The one-time chair
stipend may be claimed once every 24 months.

Claims above RM 1,500 require additional HR approval before purchase. The
assistant may explain this policy and help prepare a claim, but it cannot
approve claims — a manager or HR decides.

# 4. Returning equipment

On leaving the company, or when an FWA ends and the employee returns to the
office full-time, company-owned equipment (laptop, monitor) must be returned
within 7 working days. The chair stipend is not repayable.

Failure to return company equipment may result in deduction from final pay
where permitted by law.
